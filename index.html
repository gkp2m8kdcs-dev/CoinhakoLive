Frequently Asked Questions

Does CoinhakoLive connect to my exchange?

No. CoinhakoLive does not connect to any exchange, brokerage, or wallet service. It is a read-only investment journal. You add your own transactions manually or import them via CSV.

Where is my data stored?

All data is stored locally on your iOS device using secure on-device storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Removing the app will permanently delete all stored data — please export a CSV backup first if you wish to retain it.

Can I export my data?

Yes. From the Settings tab you can export your transactions, watchlist, and preferences to CSV at any time. The export is generated entirely on-device.

Does CoinhakoLive provide financial advice?

No. All educational content, insights, and analytics are informational only and do not constitute financial advice. Always do your own research before making investment decisions.

Is CoinhakoLive a trading app?

No. CoinhakoLive is a portfolio tracking and journaling tool. It does not place, route, or execute any trades and is compliant with Apple's Guideline 5.6 for non-trading crypto apps.

Which iOS versions are supported?

CoinhakoLive is built natively in SwiftUI for iOS. Please check the App Store listing for the current minimum iOS version.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Send an email to the address in the Contact section below. Please include your iOS version, device model, and a clear description of the issue or suggestion.
