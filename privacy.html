Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Short version: CoinhakoLive does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. All your data stays on your iPhone.

1. Introduction

CoinhakoLive ("the App", "we", "our") is a privacy-first crypto investment journal built natively for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is handled, and what choices you have. By using CoinhakoLive, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

CoinhakoLive is designed so that no personal data leaves your device. Specifically, we do not:

  • Require an account, login, or registration of any kind
  • Collect names, email addresses, or contact information through the App
  • Track your identity, location, or device identifiers for advertising
  • Sync your portfolio, transactions, or watchlist to any remote server
  • Share any data with third parties, analytics services, brokers, or exchanges
  • Use cookies, pixels, or similar tracking technologies inside the App

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following information only on your iOS device using standard Apple APIs. None of it is sent to us or to any third party:

  • Transactions you add (asset, quantity, price, fees, dates, notes)
  • Holdings and portfolio composition derived from your transactions
  • Watchlists and custom tags
  • App preferences (theme, default currency, display options)
  • Optional CSV exports you generate yourself

This data is sandboxed within the App and protected by iOS app sandboxing. You can remove it at any time by uninstalling the App or by using the in-app "Delete All Data" option.

4. Market Data

CoinhakoLive displays market information (prices, charts, sector categorization, sentiment indicators) sourced from publicly available crypto market aggregators. These public data services may see anonymous request metadata (such as your IP address) when the App fetches market information. No identifiers tied to your portfolio, transactions, or personal identity are sent with these requests.

5. Email Communications

If you voluntarily contact us by email at the address below, we will receive and retain only the information you choose to send us (your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments). We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and to improve the App. You may ask us to delete any email correspondence at any time.

6. Children's Privacy

CoinhakoLive is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, this policy applies equally to all users.

7. Data Security

All local data is protected by iOS platform security (sandboxing, file-system permissions, and at-rest encryption of the device). Because your data never leaves your device, there is no remote attack surface tied to your portfolio. We strongly recommend keeping your device passcode and Face ID / Touch ID enabled.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Because CoinhakoLive does not maintain any server-side profile about you, most traditional data rights are satisfied by the on-device design. You can at any time:

  • Export all of your data to CSV from inside the App
  • Edit or delete individual transactions, holdings, or watchlist items
  • Reset the App entirely from the Settings screen
  • Uninstall the App to permanently remove all local data
  • Request deletion of any email correspondence by contacting us

9. International Users

CoinhakoLive is operated from Türkiye and made available worldwide through the Apple App Store. No personal data is transferred across borders because no personal data leaves your device in the first place.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Material changes will also be announced through an in-app notice where appropriate. Continued use of the App after a change indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
